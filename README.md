# Privacy Policy for Campusio

**Last updated: February 1, 2026**

## Introduction

Welcome to **Campusio**. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard information when you use the Campusio mobile application available on Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

By using Campusio, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected Information

#### Anonymous Device Identifier

* When you first use Campusio, we generate a **random, anonymous device identifier**
* This identifier is stored locally on your device
* It is **not** a hardware identifier and cannot be used to identify you personally
* It is used only to associate app activity (such as feedback) with a device

#### Push Notification Token

* If you enable push notifications, we collect a **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token**
* This token is used **only** to deliver notifications to your device

---

### Information You Provide

#### AI Chat and Explain Queries

* When you use Campusio’s AI Chat or Explain features, the text you enter is sent to our servers to generate responses
* Queries and AI responses may be stored to improve the quality and accuracy of the service

#### Feedback

* When you rate an AI response (thumbs up/down) or submit optional comments, this feedback is sent to our servers
* Feedback is associated only with your anonymous device identifier and session identifier

#### App Feedback

* If you submit general feedback about the app, your message is sent to our servers
* Providing an email address is **optional** and used only to respond to your inquiry

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information only to:

* Provide AI-powered campus and academic explanations
* Deliver push notifications you opt into
* Improve AI responses and overall app experience
* Analyze feedback and usage patterns in aggregate
* Personalize content using locally stored preferences
* Respond to feedback or support requests you submit

---

## Data Storage and Security

* **Local Storage**: Anonymous device identifier, preferences, and settings are stored securely on your device
* **Server Storage**: Chat queries, AI responses, and feedback are stored on our servers
* **Security Measures**: We use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect data
* **Encryption**: All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS

---

## Third-Party Services

Campusio uses the following third-party services:

### Firebase (Google)

* Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for push notifications
* Firebase Core for app infrastructure
* Firebase may collect certain device information as described in
  [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

---

## Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your information.

We may share data only:

* With service providers (Firebase, Railway) necessary to operate the app
* To comply with legal obligations or lawful requests
* To protect user safety, rights, or public safety when required by law

---

## Data Retention

* AI chat data and feedback are retained only as long as necessary to improve the service
* Push notification tokens are retained while notifications are enabled
* Local data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app

---

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

* Disable push notifications via device settings
* Clear local data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app
* Submit feedback anonymously
* Request deletion of server-stored data linked to your anonymous device identifier

---

## Children's Privacy

Campusio is intended for college and university students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us immediately.
